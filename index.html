How it works: your phone's GPS drops a waypoint roughly every 5 meters you walk — no cell signal or map data needed, just open sky. In Return Mode, Backtrail walks you back along that exact chain of points, one at a time, until you're back at your start.

Keep this screen open while tracking. This app will try to stop your screen from auto-dimming, but manually pressing the power button pauses tracking completely — that's an Android/iOS rule for web pages, not something this app can override.
